# 第1条信经 - 灵修 02

## 灵修标题

灵修默想 2

## 英文原文

```
The First Article
"...maker of heaven and earth..."
"I have the answer. What's your question?" said the
bumper sticker on the car ahead of me on the freeway.
How typical of man to think he has the answer for
everything—and as usual, his answers lie on the hori-
zontal, not the vertical, plane, pointing not to God but
only to himself. Ask about life's purpose and his
answers point to his own pleasure or promotion. Ask
about reaching eternity, and his answers involve his
own sweating and striving. Ask about his origin—where
he and all around him came from—and the answers
still refuse to look up to the Almighty. But not so with
those who by divine grace call God their "Father
Almighty." For them there's marvelous meaning in the
expression "Maker of heaven and earth."
```

## 中文翻译

第一条
"……天地的造物主……"

"我有答案。你的问题是什么？"高速公路前面一辆车的保险杠贴纸上说着。这是多么典型的人的想法啊——他以为他对一切都有答案——而且通常，他的答案在水平平面上，而不是竖直平面上，指向的不是上帝而只是他自己。问关于生命的目的，他的答案指向他自己的快乐或升迁。问关于到达永恒，他的答案涉及他自己的汗水和奋斗。问关于他的起源——他和他周围的一切从哪里来——答案仍然拒绝抬头看向全能者。但是那些因神圣恩典而称上帝为"全能的父"的人则不同。对他们来说，"天地的造物主"这个表述有奇妙的意义。
